
 Ebeano Supermarket – Cybersecurity Consulting Project

Project Overview

This project presents a simulated cybersecurity consulting engagement for Ebeano Supermarket, a growing retail business with multiple outlets in Africa.

The business uses cloud-based Point-of-Sale (POS) systems, staff tablets, customer Wi-Fi, kitchen terminals, and social media platforms. The scenario highlights cybersecurity risks arising from shared passwords, unattended devices, ransomware threats, phishing, and potential POS payment diversion.

The objective is to develop a practical, affordable cybersecurity improvement plan that protects business operations, customer information, and financial transactions.

Project Objectives

- Develop an Identity and Access Management (IAM) policy for business systems.
- Design a secure network architecture that separates guest Wi-Fi from operational systems.
- Create realistic phishing awareness scenarios and a staff training script.
- Recommend physical security measures for tablets and POS devices.
- Identify three priority cybersecurity risks and propose cost-effective mitigations.

 Project Scope

This consulting project covers five deliverables:

1. Identity and Access Management (IAM) Policy
2. Network Security Plan
3. Social Engineering Defense and Staff Training
4. Physical Security Measures
5. Top Three Risks and Mitigation Strategies

 Business Context

The supermarket has 28 staff members across management, kitchen operations, floor sales, cashier operations, and social media marketing.

Security concerns include weak shared passwords, unsecured staff tablets, ransomware exposure, phishing attempts, and the risk of POS device substitution or payment diversion.

Project Approach

The proposed security improvement plan focuses on risk-based prioritization, least-privilege access, network segmentation, employee awareness, physical device protection, and affordable security controls.
