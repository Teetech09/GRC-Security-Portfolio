Risk Register

Overview

This folder contains the simulated cybersecurity risk register for **NorthBridge Financial Services Ltd.**, a fictional Canadian financial services organisation.

The risk register documents identified cybersecurity risks, evaluates their potential likelihood and impact, records existing security controls, and recommends appropriate risk treatment actions.

Objectives

- Identify key cybersecurity risks affecting the organisation.
- Assess inherent and residual risk levels.
- Document existing security controls and control gaps.
- Assign risk ownership and accountability.
- Recommend risk treatment actions.
- Support risk monitoring and management decision-making.

Files in This Folder

| File | Description |
|---|---|
|`risk_register.csv | Documents identified cybersecurity risks, risk scores, existing controls, owners and treatment recommendations. |

Risk Assessment Approach

The project uses a qualitative risk assessment methodology.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

Both likelihood and impact are rated on a scale of 1–5. The resulting score determines the risk rating.

Risk ratings are classified as Low, Medium, High or Critical, according to the scoring methodology documented in this folder.

Scope of Risk Assessment

The assessment considers risks relating to:

- Identity and access management.
- Phishing and security awareness.
- Cloud security and data protection.
- Ransomware and incident response.
- Security monitoring and logging.
- Third-party and vendor security.
- Backup and disaster recovery.
- Vulnerability and patch management.

Important Disclaimer

This is a simulated GRC portfolio project developed for professional learning and demonstration purposes. NorthBridge Financial Services Ltd. is fictional, and all risks, assessments, scores, controls, observations and recommendations are illustrative. They do not represent an actual audit or assessment of a real organisation.
